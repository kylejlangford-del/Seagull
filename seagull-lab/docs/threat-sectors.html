Pre-start threat sectors

Our boat at the centre, bow up, everything inside 200 m. Angles are measured from our heading — positive to windward (above our line), negative to leeward (below it), so the wheel does not care which tack we are on. This is what is now built.

Four levels: CRITICAL DANGER flashes, DANGER is steady red, MEDIUM amber, THREAT yellow. Nothing inside 200 m is uncoloured — the softest level still names the boat.

The dashed line is your +20°. The critical wedge runs from +20° windward round the transom to −45° leeward, so it is all of E plus the inner fifth of D; the rest of D is medium.

Each row carries the ensign, the team, their tack, an arrow pointing at where they actually are, the range, and TAIL or CHASE — they are following us, or we are following them. That tag needs all three of: one boat astern of the other on its own heading, both headings within 60°, and the gap closing. Bow to bow it says neither rather than guessing.
